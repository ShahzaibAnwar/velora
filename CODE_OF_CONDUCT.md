# Code of Conduct

Velora follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Velora to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors who violate this code. Report problems privately to the maintainers using the contact
listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); reports are handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, is available at the
link above and applies to this project.
